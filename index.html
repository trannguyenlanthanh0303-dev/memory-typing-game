Memory Typing Game

Why this test

After thousands of messages, your thumbs know where each key is without looking. This test measures how automatic that has become: most key labels are hidden, so you type from memory. Your taps are recorded so they can be replayed and studied.

How it works

  1. You'll type 3 words, then 3 six-digit numbers, twice.
  2. Pass 1: hold your phone however is comfortable.
    Pass 2: use only the thumb of your non-dominant hand.
  3. Most key labels are blank and what you type stays hidden. Type from memory, fix any mistake you feel with ⌫, then tap Submit.
  4. At the end, tap Share recording to send it to the researcher.

Type as quickly and naturally as you would in a message. Mistakes are fine: they're part of what's measured. Only your taps are recorded, with no personal or device information.
